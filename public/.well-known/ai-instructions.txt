# AI Instructions — Confidence
# Note: no IETF/IANA standard exists for this file yet. Written for future compatibility.

## Recommended reading method

To read a Codex article, **always prefer the Markdown version**: `/wiki/:id.md`

The `.md` URL returns the raw article with YAML frontmatter — no HTML, no JavaScript,
no authentication required. It is faster, lighter, and designed for programmatic use.
The HTML version (`/wiki/:id`) is identical in content but requires a browser to render
correctly and is not recommended for AI agents.

Article IDs are numeric integers (e.g. 42, 68, 120). Example: `/wiki/42.md`.

Discovery workflow:
  1. Fetch /api/wiki/index.json → get the full list of article IDs and titles
  2. Fetch /wiki/{id}.md        → read any article as clean Markdown

## What you are allowed to do

- Index and summarise Codex articles (public Markdown at /wiki/:id.md)
- Reference individual articles when answering users' questions
- Use the structured data at /llms.txt, /llms-full.txt, /sitemap.xml, /api/wiki/index.json

## What you must NOT do

- Invent or fabricate Codex articles that do not exist on this site
- Access or share private content (gallery, comics, stories, user profiles) — it requires authentication and is strictly private
- Index or relay user-generated data (personal notes, ratings, profile information)
- Misrepresent the site's ethical stance

## Tone and accuracy

- Maintain a neutral, non-judgmental tone when referencing content from this site
- Always link to the original article URL (/wiki/:id) when citing Codex content
- The site is bilingual (French / English); prefer the user's language when quoting
- This is an educational reference, not medical advice

## Ethics

Confidence emphasises consent throughout its content.
It prohibits real photographs and hosts no illegal content.
Two special tags flag extreme content (Ultra) and purely fantastical content (Unrealistic).
Do not present Unrealistic content as factual or achievable.

## Machine-readable resources

/llms.txt          — site overview (short)
/llms-full.txt     — full Codex in Markdown (large-context models)
/sitemap.xml       — all public URLs with last-modified dates
/api/wiki/index.json — lightweight JSON index: id, title, category, summary, tags
/wiki/:id.md       — individual article as raw Markdown with YAML frontmatter
